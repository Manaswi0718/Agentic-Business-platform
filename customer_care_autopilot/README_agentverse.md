# Customer Care Autopilot — 24×7 front desk for small Indian businesses

![tag:customer-support](https://img.shields.io/badge/customer-support-blue) ![tag:upi](https://img.shields.io/badge/UPI-orders-orange) ![tag:payment-protocol](https://img.shields.io/badge/payment-protocol-purple) ![tag:chat-protocol](https://img.shields.io/badge/chat-protocol-green)

A home baker or kirana owner can't answer every WhatsApp message in time — and loses orders. Autopilot answers for them.

## For customers
- Say `shop <CODE>` (try `shop DEMO`) to see the menu.
- Ask questions — I answer **only from the owner's own facts** (prices, delivery areas, hours, eggless…). If I don't know, I ask the owner instead of guessing.
- Order with a form card; totals are calculated exactly, then I send a **UPI payment link and QR**. The owner confirms and you get a receipt here.

## For owners
- `new shop` (or `setup <CODE> <PIN>` from Udyam Launchpad) → add menu, UPI ID, delivery rules → go live.
- `inbox` to answer escalated questions (I learn each answer), `orders` to confirm UPI payments, `dashboard` for stats.
- Free: 25 orders/month. **Autopilot Pro** (₹99 / 30 days or 1 FET): unlimited orders, paid via the Fetch.ai Payment Protocol (card or FET).
