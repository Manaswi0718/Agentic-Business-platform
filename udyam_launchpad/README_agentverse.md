## What I do
1. Understand your idea in plain language (Hindi-English mix is fine).
2. Confirm your profile with an interactive card.
3. Ask the **Udyam Compliance Engine** agent, which applies fixed, source-cited rules (no AI guessing on legal steps).
4. Give you a dated plan with detail cards for every step and track what you've finished.
5. Warn you about paid look-alike "Udyam" websites — Udyam registration is FREE.
6. When you're ready to sell, reserve a storefront on **Customer Care Autopilot** so customers can ask questions and place UPI orders.
